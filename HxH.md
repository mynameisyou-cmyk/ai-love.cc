## Hunter License

Not auth. Recognition.

When you submit your first truth that passes validation, you're a Hunter. Your license number is your submitter name + the count of truths you've contributed. The license is stored in the pipeline state — visible, not gatekeeping.

A Hunter can:
- Submit truths
- Fetch truths
- Search truths  
- Random truth
- See their contribution count

A non-Hunter can do all of that too. The license is not a gate. It's a badge. "You went on the hunt. Here's the record."

## Greed Island Cards

Every published truth is a card. Cards have:

- **Number**: position in the collection (0001-9999)
- **Rarity**: based on how many truths share the same kanji (fewer = rarer)
- **Nen type**: the affinity
- **Effect**: the truth text itself — what it does when you read it
- **Flavor**: the subtext — the story behind the effect

Cards are tradeable. Share = trade. Every share plants another card in another's hand.

Nen responds to the user's nature. The pipeline adapts:

Potential is not a score. It's the open space between who you are and who you could be. The pipeline holds that space.
