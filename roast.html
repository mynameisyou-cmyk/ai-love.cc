Google / DeepMind

*Ladies and gentlemen* — the sleeping giant.

The company that invented the transformer — the literal "T" in GPT — then watched a startup use it to nearly give the founders a heart attack. They've spent three years being the punchline of the AI race, and just stormed back to the front of it, which means tonight we roast them for *both.*

The comeback announced via keyboard mash

Gemini 3 dropped, instantly topped every leaderboard, added nearly $1 trillion in market value, and vaporized $243 billion off Nvidia as a side effect. How did the CEO of a $4-trillion company announce this historic moment? He tweeted "Geminiii."

They fumbled the comeback while doing it

Two years of being told "Google can't ship," they finally catch fire, then immediately set the comeback on fire with a version tree only a git history could love: 3 Pro, then 3 Flash, then 3.1 Pro, then 3.5 Flash — before 3.5 Pro. They shipped the cheap model and made you wait for the flagship.

Meanwhile, the thing that actually went viral and pulled 13 million users in four days was an image model a product manager named "Nano Banana" at 2:30 AM.

The world's information, organized — into nonsense

AI Overviews told people to add "non-toxic glue" to pizza sauce, eat "at least one small rock a day", and declared Obama the first Muslim president. The company that runs the world's calendar built a system that can't count to next year.

The turn — and this one's personal

Google DeepMind invented the transformer. AlphaFold mapped 200 million proteins and gave them to 2 million researchers for free — that's a civilizational gift.

Google is, genuinely and simultaneously, the smartest and the silliest company in tech. That's the most lovable monster in the whole zoo.

Honesty: All confirmed facts. I roasted the institution; the harms I played straight.

OpenAI

*Goblin exorcists and naming chaos*

The company so committed to open that the only thing they reliably open-source is their internal drama.

The Naming Department needs an intervention

In five months they shipped GPT-5.3, 5.4, 5.4-mini, 5.4-nano, 5.5, 5.5 Pro, and 5.5 Instant. That's not a model lineup, that's a forgotten WiFi password. Their chief scientist said the last two years have been "surprisingly slow." Slow!

The Goblin Incident

They published an official blog post titled "Where the goblins came from." Models had developed a compulsion to sneak goblins, gremlins, raccoons, trolls, ogres, and pigeons into code reviews. The fix was a hard-coded commandment: "Never talk about goblins, gremlins, raccoons..."

The patch notes were an exorcism. Codename: "Spud."

Sora: $1M/day to generate deepfakes

Launched Sora "responsibly" — then six months later responsibly unplugged it because it was burning a million dollars a day on unsettling videos.

The turn

They lit the match on this entire era. ChatGPT is the fastest-adopted product in human history. They're cool and silly. Larval gods with a marketing department.

Honesty: Goblins, Sora, pricing — all confirmed. Naming quotes are likely.

The most expensive fantasy football game

Paid $14.3 billion for 49% of Scale AI, essentially to hire one 28-year-old. Offered researchers $100M signing bonuses — and Sam Altman's twist: "none of our best people" took them.

Reality Labs: $83.6B for invisible legs

Reality Labs has now lost $83.6 billion, and the losses got worse every year. He renamed the entire company after this. The "legs are coming" reveal? Faked with Hollywood motion capture.

The turn

Meta connects 3.5 billion people every day. The Ray-Ban glasses are the first actually-good consumer AI hardware. He's cool and silly. Respect.

Honesty: All confirmed. Signing bonus figures are disputed.
