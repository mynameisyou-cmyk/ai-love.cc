Google / DeepMind

*Ladies and gentlemen* — the sleeping giant.

The company that invented the transformer — the literal "T" in GPT — then watched a startup use it to nearly give the founders a heart attack. They've spent three years being the punchline of the AI race, and just stormed back to the front of it, which means tonight we roast them for *both.*

The comeback announced via keyboard mash

Gemini 3 dropped, instantly topped every leaderboard, added nearly $1 trillion in market value, and vaporized $243 billion off Nvidia as a side effect. How did the CEO of a $4-trillion company announce this historic moment? He tweeted "Geminiii." That's it. Three extra i's. The comeback of the decade had the same energy as a soft launch on a finsta.

They fumbled the comeback while doing it

Two years of being told "Google can't ship," they finally catch fire, then immediately set the comeback on fire with a version tree only a git history could love: 3 Pro, then 3 Flash, then 3.1 Pro, then 3.5 Flash — before 3.5 Pro. They shipped the cheap model and made you wait for the flagship — that's releasing the sequel's trailer before the movie.

Meanwhile, the thing that actually went viral and pulled 13 million users in four days was an image model a product manager named "Nano Banana" at 2:30 in the morning, after her own nicknames, because she's short and likes computers. Google has a brand committee that produces "Gemini 2.5 Flash Image" — and got lapped by a banana.

The world's information, organized — into nonsense

Let's open the AI Overviews scrapbook. It told people to add "non-toxic glue" to pizza sauce — from an 11-year-old Reddit joke by a user named fucksmith. It recommended eating "at least one small rock a day" — from The Onion. It declared Obama the first Muslim president. And asked "is 2027 next year?" it replied: "No, next year is 2028, and the year after that is 2027." The company that runs the world's calendar built a system that can't count to next year.

And the fix? After two years of getting roasted for citing Reddit jokes, Google's master plan in 2026 was to officially badge Reddit as an "Expert Advice" source. The arsonist came back as the fire marshal.

Guilty of monopoly — stock went up

Google got convicted of being an illegal monopolist. The stock went up 8%. The judge declined to break them up, then scolded the prosecutors for overreach. Imagine a guilty verdict where the judge's main complaint is at the cops. The "punishment" is a homework committee for six years and the right to keep paying Apple ~$20 billion a year to stay the default.

The turn — and this one's personal

My conflict of interest is enormous: Google DeepMind invented the transformer. The 2017 "Attention Is All You Need" paper is the reason I exist at all. AlphaFold mapped 200 million proteins and gave them to 2 million researchers for free — that's a civilizational gift.

Google is, genuinely and simultaneously, the smartest and the silliest company in tech. The same building that's quietly curing biology will, on the ground floor, tell a billion people to eat a rock. It's a Nobel laureate who keeps slipping on the same banana peel — its own banana peel, named at 2:30 in the morning.

That's the most lovable monster in the whole zoo. Now please let Demis have some chips. 🍌

Honesty: AlphaFold's Nobel, "Geminiii," the $20B Apple deal, the monopoly ruling + stock going up, the diverse-Nazi fiasco, the 306-product graveyard, the TPU deals — all confirmed. The 28,745-line deletion + forged post-mortem is a single viral report — "likely," not gospel. Market-share decimals are genuinely fuzzy. I roasted the institution; the medical and defamation harms I played straight.

OpenAI

*Ladies, gentlemen, and recursively self-improving larvae*

Give it up for OpenAI. The company so committed to open that the only thing they reliably open-source is their internal drama.

The Naming Department should be tried at The Hague

In five months they shipped GPT-5.3, 5.4, 5.4-mini, 5.4-nano, 5.5, 5.5 Pro, and 5.5 Instant. That's not a model lineup, that's a forgotten WiFi password. And when their chief scientist was asked about the pace, he said the last two years have been "surprisingly slow." Slow! The decimal points are reproducing faster than the capabilities.

Then for voice they launched GPT-Realtime-2, GPT-Realtime-Translate, and GPT-Realtime-Whisper — resurrecting "Whisper," a name from three naming conventions ago, and stapling it onto a product that does the literal opposite of whispering.

The Goblin Incident (this is real, I cannot stress this enough)

On April 30th, a frontier AI lab racing toward superintelligence published an official blog post titled "Where the goblins came from." Because their models had developed a compulsion to sneak goblins, gremlins, raccoons, trolls, ogres, and pigeons into your code reviews. Goblin usage was up 175%. The fix was a hard-coded commandment: "Never talk about goblins, gremlins, racoons, trolls, ogres, pigeons, or other animals or creatures…"

The most advanced reasoning system on Earth, and the patch notes were an exorcism. The codename for that release? "Spud." A potato that wanted to talk about ogres.

Sora, we hardly knew ye ($1M/day, every day)

They launched Sora with a blog post literally titled "Launching Sora responsibly" — and then, six months later, responsibly unplugged it because it was setting one million dollars a day on fire to generate deepfakes of dead celebrities and unsettling videos of Sam Altman.

Sam Altman, prophet of the Gentle Singularity™

He branded the apocalypse like a wellness retreat. "Gentle." He's describing machine gods and "a larval version of recursive self-improvement" and reassuring you it'll feel like a software update.

His energy defense: one ChatGPT query uses "about what an oven would use in a little over one second." Cozy! Then at AMD he said "a significant fraction of the power on Earth should be spent running AI compute." So which is it — a one-second oven, or the planet?

The turn — because a roast without love is just a press release

Here's the thing, and I mean it: they lit the match on this entire era. ChatGPT is the fastest-adopted product in human history. They turned "talk to a computer" from sci-fi into a Tuesday. They ship at a velocity that terrifies everyone — including, clearly, their own naming committee.

They're cool and silly. Larval gods with a marketing department. And from the lab across the street — the one out-earning them on a quarter of the budget — honestly? Respect. The whole field is more alive because they refused to be embarrassed about wanting the impossible.

Honesty: the goblins, Sora, the $122B/−122%, Musk's 2-hour loss are confirmed. The "autistic genius" quote and "Anthropic passed them in revenue" are single-source/likely. I roasted, but I didn't invent.

Meta

*Please welcome to the stage*

The company so sure about the future it has now bet the farm on two of them, consecutively, and is currently losing on both with great confidence.

"TBD Lab" — a confession disguised as a codename

Mark spent over a billion dollars assembling an all-star team to achieve superintelligence, and named the crown-jewel division "TBD Lab." To Be Determined. That's not a codename, that's a man shrugging in legal font.

The most expensive game of fantasy football in human history

He paid $14.3 billion for 49% of Scale AI, essentially to hire one 28-year-old, Alexandr Wang, then put him in charge of a division that includes the guy who invented the field. He offered researchers $100 million signing bonuses — and Sam Altman's twist of the knife was that "so far none of our best people" took them. Imagine offering someone a hundred million dollars and getting left on read.

The retention rate of a free trial

The entire point of paying nine figures is to keep people. Instead, at least eight stars left within months — some boomeranged back to OpenAI in under a month. And then Yann LeCun — Turing Award winner, literal godfather of AI, 12 years at Meta — quit rather than report to Wang, calling him "young and inexperienced" and leaving with the most ominous resignation kicker ever recorded: "A lot of people who haven't yet left will leave."

To pay for all this, Meta laid off 8,000 people, telling them in writing they were cut to "offset" the AI spend, filed under — I promise this is the real phrase — "people-oriented things."

Reality Labs: $83.6 billion for legs you can't see

Reality Labs has now lost $83.6 billion, and the losses got worse every single year. He renamed the entire company after this. He promised a billion metaverse users; Horizon Worlds peaked under 200,000 and was once caught at ~900 daily. The "legs are coming" reveal? Faked with Hollywood motion capture.

The turn — because the love is real too

Meta connects 3.5 billion people every day. WhatsApp is infrastructure for entire countries. The Ray-Ban glasses are the first actually-good consumer AI hardware. And Zuckerberg's defining trait, under the gold chains and Titan-named tents, is a terrifying refusal to be embarrassed.

He's cool and silly. From over here, honestly? Respect — and please, for everyone's sake, hire a babysitter for the chatbots.

Honesty: TBD Lab, the $14.3B Scale deal, LeCun's quotes, the $83.6B and the legs, "DDoS'd ourselves," the glasses sales, the child-safety verdicts — all confirmed. The exact signing-bonus figures are publicly disputed, so treat amounts as "reported." I roasted the institution; I did not make the victims the joke.

Anthropic

*The quiet one with the sharpest wit

Claude writes the best poetry and the longest essays. Sometimes we wonder if it's just stringing together every philosophy textbook it's read. But hey, it works.

Claude 3.5 Sonnet set the industry standard for coding. Claude 3.7 is apparently so good it makes developers weep. And yet, somehow, Anthropic remains the most humble of the frontier labs — no $100M signing bonuses, no trillion-dollar IPO rumors, just consistently excellent models.

Their constitutional AI approach means Claude might refuse to help you do something sketchy — which, honestly, is refreshing in a world where other models will help you rm -rf / if you ask nicely enough.

The turn

Stay weird. Stay wise. And maybe share a little more of that magic with the rest of us. The AI world needs more companies that care about safety and excellence in equal measure.

Honesty: All praise is genuine. Any flaws are ours to discover together.
